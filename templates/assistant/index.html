    Your assistant, explained.

    Write a question and choose Send question. The answer comes from Google's Gemini AI. Each question is sent independently, without your earlier messages.

    Your questions and answers stay only on this page. Refresh or choose Clear to remove them. Don't send passwords, private documents, or other sensitive information. Questions are sent to Google for processing.

    Install it like an app

    If your browser offers installation, use Install app here or the install icon in the address bar. In Edge, look under … → Apps; in Chrome, look under ⋮ → Cast, save, and share. On iPhone, use Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen.

    When you're offline

    After an online visit, this interface can open without internet. You can draft a question, but generating an answer still needs a connection. Failed questions are not queued or sent automatically.
